2. Torque-Speed Quadrants

2b. How to Read the Demo

The top studio uses an intentionally simplified educational model so the mode changes are easy to see.

  • Iq≈T_cmd/Kt, so torque sign directly changes q-axis current sign.
  • Id stays near zero below base speed and becomes negative in field weakening.
  • ωdot=(T_cmd−T_load−grade torque)/J, so road grade and load change acceleration.
  • Limit flags are illustrative: traction, regen clipping, field weakening, thermal/fault cases.

Production validation must use calibrated PMSM, inverter, battery, tire, brake-blending and thermal models.

3. Engineering Notes

Sign convention

Positive vehicle direction is forward. Positive motor speed corresponds to forward travel through the fixed gear. Positive torque accelerates positive rotation. P_mech = T_e · ω_m: positive means motoring, negative means regeneration.

What to monitor

Log Id/Iq, phase currents, rotor angle, estimated torque, speed, DC bus voltage/current, battery charge limit, modulation index, temperature derates, wheel slip, and mode/fault flags.

Common misses

Reverse regeneration, zero-speed torque, grade hold heating, resolver sign errors, current sensor offset, DC bus overvoltage in regen, and integrator windup during torque clipping.
